How to use this handbook

Purpose and scope

This handbook explains every active owner-side module in the UNIQA SUITES system. It focuses on what each page is for, what the Owner can control, how information flows between modules, and which decisions require extra care.

Owner access. The Owner role has full access to all active operational and administrative pages. Staff see only the pages and actions assigned to them. “Import & Data Quality” has been retired and is intentionally excluded from this handbook.

Core control principles

  • Availability is authoritative. Holds, confirmed stays, room blocks and safety closures prevent overlapping occupancy.
  • Confirmed prices are snapshots. Later changes to rates or policies affect new quotes and repriced bookings, not an already confirmed price.
  • Evidence is not settlement. A payment screenshot is recorded first and counts only after verification. A platform-paid booking becomes cash only when the channel payout arrives.
  • Corrections preserve history. Financial mistakes are reversed with a new opposite entry; originals remain visible.
  • Permissions follow the person. Pages and sensitive actions can be granted individually. Owners and Administrators retain full access.

The Dashboard is the Owner’s control center. It summarizes current activity, highlights matters requiring attention, and provides performance views without changing underlying records.

Dashboard tabOwner interpretation

Chapters 3–6

Bookings and guest management

Booking Calendar

Purpose: A visual timeline of room occupancy, holds, blocks and operational status.

  • Move between date ranges and properties, then read each room across the timeline.
  • Click a stay to open the booking. Click an empty date to begin a booking for that room.
  • Use room blocks for maintenance, owner use or other periods when a room must not be sold.
  • Housekeeping indicators show whether a room is ready, dirty, in progress or awaiting inspection.

The calendar is a planning view; booking, hold and room-block records are the controls that actually protect dates.

Reservations & Inquiries

Purpose: The complete reservation register, from first inquiry through checkout or cancellation.

  • Inquiry: records interest but does not reserve dates.
  • Tentative/hold: protects selected dates until the configured expiry time.
  • Confirmed: commits the room and stores the price snapshot.
  • Checked in / checked out: controls the live stay and triggers turnover work.
  • Cancelled / no-show: records the outcome while preserving history.

The booking detail page brings together stay information, charges, balance, receipts, refunds, parking, copy-ready guest messages and the complete status history. Amend dates, rooms or party size through the booking action so conflicts and repricing are rechecked.

Quotes & Messages

Purpose: Calculate an accurate proposal for available rooms and create consistent guest-facing wording.

  1. Enter stay dates, guest count, source and optional extras.
  2. Review every vacant unit and the calculated price; unavailable units state the reason.
  3. Select the preferred room, inspect the line-by-line estimate, then save the quote.
  4. Copy the message for Messenger or produce the quote PDF.
  5. Create a booking when the guest proceeds.

Updating a saved quote creates a new version under the same reference. Earlier versions remain available. A quote alone never reserves a room.

Guests

Purpose: Maintain reusable guest contact and identity information with links to booking history.

  • Search by name or contact details; open a guest record to review related stays.
  • Keep one accurate profile per guest wherever possible to avoid fragmented history.
  • Update contact information carefully; do not place sensitive data in general notes unless operationally necessary.

Chapters 8–11

Property operations

Cleaning

Purpose: Plan, assign, perform and inspect turnover or manual cleaning tasks.

  • View work by date and stage: unassigned, assigned, in progress, awaiting inspection and finished.
  • Create or plan a task, assign a cleaner manually, or distribute tasks by current workload.
  • Cleaners start and finish work, complete the checklist, add photos and record supplies used.
  • Inspection passes the room to ready status. A failed inspection returns the task with the reason.
  • Urgency and due time help prioritize arriving rooms.

Laundry

Purpose: Track linen work associated with checkout and other operational needs.

When automatic suggestion is enabled, checkout proposes a laundry job. Staff then record quantity, provider, status and cost. Laundry cost remains operationally separate from room readiness unless staff link the workflow through their procedures.

Maintenance & Damage

Purpose: Separate repair work from guest-related incidents while keeping a documented decision trail.

  • Work orders: assign a repair, severity, responsible person, status, cost and notes. Safety-critical work can stop a room from being sold or marked ready.
  • Damage & incidents: document the room, item, event, evidence and communications. Reporting an incident does not automatically charge a guest.
  • Owner decision: approve or reject damage outcomes only after reviewing evidence and the communication history.

Inventory & Refills

Purpose: Maintain stock-on-hand for amenities, cleaning materials, linen and consumables.

  • Add an item with its unit and reorder level.
  • Record every receipt, issue, consumption or correction as a movement.
  • Cleaning-task consumption reduces stock only when the user records it.
  • Review low-stock alerts before they become service interruptions.

Parking

Purpose: Manage parking spaces and date-bound assignments.

The list view manages spaces and assignments; the calendar shows occupancy by stall across a month. The system refuses overlapping assignments for the same space. Link assignments to the booking or clearly identify the holder and vehicle.

Chapters 12–13

Money and financial controls

Payments — Receipts

Purpose: Record money reported as received and verify it against the real account.

1 · RecordEnter booking reference, date, amount, method, receiving account, external reference and evidence.
2 · VerifyA separate authorized review confirms cleared funds or rejects the receipt with a reason.
3 · CorrectReverse an incorrect verified receipt; the system posts a negative entry and preserves the original.

Channel settlements

Purpose: Reconcile Airbnb or other platforms from gross booking value to actual bank payout.

Record provider gross, fees, adjustments and expected net separately. When funds arrive, record actual payout, date and receiving account. Any difference remains visible for investigation.

Reconciliation

Purpose: Surface exceptions and close a reviewed accounting period.

  • Review missing proof, unusual or duplicate references, unmatched amounts and unpaid channel settlements.
  • Lock a month only after the exceptions and supporting records have been reviewed.
  • A locked month accepts no silent historical edits. Corrections are new adjustments dated in an open period.

Expenses

Purpose: Record operating costs by category and allocate them to a room, property or general overhead.

  • Distinguish incurred from paid. An unpaid bill is an expense but is not yet cash out.
  • Attach proof, identify the vendor and record payment method when paid.
  • Costs at or above the configured threshold require approval before payment.
  • Reverse mistakes instead of deleting or editing financial history.

Cashbook

Purpose: Show actual money movement for a selected date range.

Money in includes verified receipts and channel payouts received. Money out includes paid expenses and executed refunds. The page summarizes inflow, outflow and net movement.

Chapters 14–16

Reporting, people and governance

Reports & Analytics

Purpose: Analyze performance using an explicit period, scope and date basis.

  • Filter by dates, property, room, booking source and report type as available.
  • Use the displayed definition to understand whether figures follow stay date, receipt date, payout date, created date or expense date.
  • Review KPI cards, 12-month trends, room/channel rankings and the detailed table.
  • Export authorized reports to PDF, CSV or Excel-compatible formats for complete datasets.

Different report bases answer different questions. A stay-date revenue report should not be forced to reconcile with receipt-date cash collections.

Staff Advances

Purpose: Track cash advanced to employees and repayments against each balance.

Add the employee record, record each advance with date and reason, then post repayments as they occur. Review outstanding balances routinely and retain supporting authorization outside or attached to the organization’s approved process.

Team & Permissions

Purpose: Control who can enter the system and exactly what each person may see or do.

  • Invite a person, select a starting role, and then review every page and extra-action checkbox.
  • Grant only the access required for the person’s current duties.
  • Deactivate access promptly when a person leaves or no longer needs the system.
  • Reset two-factor enrollment only after verifying identity.
  • Temporary passwords are shown once; deliver them privately and require the user to change the password.
  • Only an Owner can change another Owner’s protected access.

Settings — Pricing and operations policy

These inputs define the organization-wide defaults used by pricing, availability, booking confirmation, housekeeping, finance, security and guest communications. Save changes only after considering their operational effect.

Effective-change principle: Policy changes apply to new calculations. Confirmed bookings retain their stored price snapshot. A later booking amendment may trigger availability checks and repricing.

Chapters 18–19

Audit, account and notifications

Audit Log

Purpose: Provide a chronological record of important actions, the person responsible and supporting details.

  • Use it to investigate changes, approvals, reversals and security-sensitive events.
  • The audit log supports accountability; it does not replace the source booking, receipt, expense or incident record.
  • Review suspicious or unexpected actions promptly and secure the affected account if necessary.

Account

Purpose: Manage the signed-in Owner’s credentials and personal preferences.

  • Change the password using a unique, private value.
  • Enable two-factor authentication, confirm the setup and store recovery codes securely.
  • Regenerate recovery codes if they may have been exposed; the old set then becomes invalid.
  • Choose which supported operational events should create personal notifications.

Notifications

Purpose: Draw attention to events that may require review.

The bell lists recent notifications and links to the relevant record. Marking an item read clears the attention state but does not resolve the underlying operational issue. Open the record, complete the required action, and use the Dashboard to confirm that the exception has cleared.

Chapter 20

Owner operating routines

Daily

  • Review Dashboard “Action needed,” arrivals, departures and room readiness.
  • Check pending receipts, outstanding balances and channel payouts received.
  • Review urgent cleaning, safety-critical repairs and inventory below reorder level.
  • Read notifications and open the underlying records.
  • Confirm near-term Airbnb or external-channel stays have been entered.

Weekly

  • Review the Booking Calendar for gaps, overlaps, extended blocks and expiring holds.
  • Inspect room and source performance, unpaid balances and expense concentration.
  • Review open incidents, repair aging, cleaning inspection failures and staff workload.
  • Check outstanding employee advances and parking conflicts.
  • Review active staff access against current duties.

Month-end

  • Resolve reconciliation exceptions and verify evidence for material transactions.
  • Confirm channel gross, fees, adjustments and actual payouts.
  • Review incurred-versus-paid expenses and execute approved refunds already sent.
  • Export and retain the required management reports.
  • Lock the month only after review; document any later correction as a new open-period adjustment.
  • Run and copy backups away from the system computer; preserve a separate secure copy of the application key.

Before changing pricing policy

  • Confirm the business decision and effective timing.
  • Check room-level rates and stay limits that may override the general setting.
  • Test common quote scenarios and read the complete line-item breakdown.
  • Brief the staff who create quotes and bookings.
  • Keep the prior policy and effective date in the Owner’s management record.
System health: Holds, alerts and scheduled reminders depend on the scheduler. Backups and the application key are management responsibilities. If system health warnings appear, resolve them before relying on unattended automation.
